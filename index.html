CSV format and how imports work

Use UTF-8 text with these columns:

id,date,description,amount,account,kind,category,refund_expected,refund_for

Use dates like 2026-09-05. Purchases have negative amounts; refunds have positive amounts. Kind is purchase, refund, or transfer. Category, refund_expected, and refund_for can be blank. Limit: 2 MB and 10,000 rows.

Reimporting the same original file reopens its saved workspace. Your original CSV stays as it was; download the reviewed CSV to keep your edits in a file. Bank exports may need their columns converted first.
